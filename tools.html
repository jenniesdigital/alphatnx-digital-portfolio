Pricing & Margin Protection Checklist for Nigerian Businesses
  • Buffer For Failed Deliveries & Returns (RTO): In Nigeria, return and exchange rates average 5%–12%. Ensure gross profit per delivered unit covers unrecoverable waybill dispatch fees.
  • Account For Payment Processor Fees: Factor in the 1.5% (+ ₦100) transaction fee charged by Paystack, Moniepoint, or card switches before setting retail prices.
  • Use Replacement Cost When Restocking: If your inventory is imported or pegged to foreign exchange, price based on what it will cost to restock tomorrow, not what you paid last month.
  • Ensure Margin Exceeds Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC): Your profit per unit must comfortably absorb the Instagram, TikTok, or Google ad spend required to win that customer.
  • Bundle Products to Increase Basket Size: Combine lower-margin essential items with high-margin accessories to raise overall order margin without increasing delivery friction.
