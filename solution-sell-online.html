THE PROBLEM & THE FIX

You shouldn't have to be physically in your shop to make a sale.

Someone could be looking for exactly what you sell right now. Maybe they need groceries. Maybe they're stocking their home. Maybe they need a particular product and don't want to drive around looking for it. Maybe they're in another part of town. Maybe they're even in another state.

But if your products aren't easy to find online, they may never know you have them. Instead, they find another shop.

Your Current Reality

And while that is happening, you're still:

"How much is this?"
"Do you have this one?"
"Please send your account number."
"Have you received the transfer?"
"Is this still available?"
  • ✕ Checking screenshots of payments.
  • ✕ Trying to remember what is in stock.
  • ✕ And manually keeping track of orders.

Your business has grown. Your systems haven't.

Imagine if your shop worked like this instead:

2 They browse your products & see what is available.

4 The order is recorded & stock is updated automatically.
5 You see what's happening without calling 3 people to ask.

That's what we're building for you.
