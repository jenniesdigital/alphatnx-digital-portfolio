Good. Keep using WhatsApp. We're not trying to take WhatsApp away from your business; we're giving it a proper system behind it. WhatsApp is excellent for conversations, but when your entire shop runs through WhatsApp, things can quickly become messy:

You're not supposed to be. You're the business owner. You know your products, you know your customers, and you know how your shop works—that's what we need from you. We handle the technology, then train you and your staff to run the system. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use your store.
