Standard Payment Fee Structures in Nigeria

Major Nigerian gateways charge standard local card and transfer processing fees:

  • Paystack / Flutterwave Local: 1.5% + ₦100 per transaction (the ₦100 fee is waived for transactions below ₦2,500), capped at ₦2,000 per transaction.
  • International Cards: 3.9% + ₦100 per transaction with settlement in USD or Naira equivalents.
  • Direct Virtual Accounts: Fixed ₦50 – ₦100 fee per transfer confirmation depending on provider SLA.

Why Multi-Gateway Routing Matters

Relying on a single payment processor risks catastrophic cart abandonment during peak sale campaigns when bank switches experience temporary network throttling. Smart checkout engines automatically fail over to secondary routes within 300ms.
