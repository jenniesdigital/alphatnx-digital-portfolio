Where Repetitive Tasks Drain Payroll

Most growing businesses do not have a revenue problem—they have a manual operations trap. As order volume scales from 20 to 150 orders a week:

  • Payment Confirmation Delays: Staff spending 5–10 minutes verifying bank transfer screenshots manually in banking apps.
  • Manual Receipt Generation: Typing order numbers, item names, and delivery addresses into PDF templates one by one.
  • Dispatch Rider Coordination: Copying customer phone numbers and directions across uncoordinated WhatsApp chats.
