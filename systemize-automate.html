Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know anything about technology? +

No. You need to understand your business. We'll handle the technical side and explain what you need in plain language.

Will you force me to use new software? +

Not necessarily. We'll look at what you're already using and determine what should stay, change, connect or be replaced.

Can you automate my existing processes? +

If the process is suitable for automation, yes. But we won't automate a broken process just because we can. First we make the process make sense. Then we automate it.

Can you build something specifically for my business? +

Yes, where the requirement calls for a custom solution. AlphaTNX already treats custom digital products such as booking platforms, customer portals and web applications as a separate custom-solutions pathway.

What if I don't know what I need? +

That's exactly why we start with the Business Systems Check. You don't need to come to us with a technical specification. Tell us what is frustrating you. We'll help map the problem.

Will my staff be able to use it? +

That is part of the point. A system that your team can't understand or use is not a successful system. We'll build around how your people actually work and provide the necessary handover/training.
