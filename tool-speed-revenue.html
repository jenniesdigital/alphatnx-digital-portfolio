How Mobile Latency Costs Nigerian Businesses

When users click your ad or social link on Instagram or Google, latency directly impacts purchase intent. If the server takes 4 seconds to deliver the first meaningful paint:

  • 53% of visits are abandoned before the hero section renders.
  • Customer acquisition costs (CAC) effectively double because paid clicks are wasted on blank screens.
  • Payment gateways trigger timeout errors when slow scripts fail to initialize before the customer switches apps.

Recommended Performance Thresholds

Target Metrics:
• First Contentful Paint (FCP): < 0.8s on 4G
• Time to Interactive (TTI): < 1.2s
• Total Page Weight: < 850KB compressed
