SPREADSHEET TEMPLATE

Multi-Location Inventory Reconciliation Template

A structured spreadsheet workbook with pre-built formulas for branch managers to record daily stock movement, batch numbers, damaged goods, and sales reconciliations.

RESOURCE SPECIFICATIONS
Format: Google Sheets & .xlsx  •  License: Free for Commercial Use

What's Included in This Resource

1. Automated Stock Depletion Formula

Automatically calculates remaining physical inventory units when sales entries are logged across retail branches.

2. Low Stock Visual Alerts

Conditional formatting highlights fast-moving SKUs before stock drops below the minimum reorder threshold.

3. Inter-Branch Transfer Log

Tracks shipments between warehouse hubs and retail branches with dispatch timestamps and receiving signatures.

4. End-of-Day Discrepancy Sheet

Calculates variance between system records and physical shelf count to detect shrinkage or data entry errors immediately.

How to Use in Your Business

1. Download the template file to your local computer or Google Drive.
2. Customize the placeholders with your business name, staff roles, and pricing rules.
3. Conduct a 20-minute training walkthrough with your team to review the workflow standards.
