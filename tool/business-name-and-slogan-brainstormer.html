What Makes a Good Business Name and Slogan?

A business name is the cornerstone of your brand identity—it is the first thing customers type into Google, search on Instagram, verify on bank transfers, and see on your invoices. A brand slogan (or tagline) works right alongside your name to communicate the specific value, speed, or quality your customers can expect.

When starting or rebranding a business in Nigeria, many founders get stuck between names that are too generic (making them impossible to register or find online) and names that are too obscure (forcing you to explain what you do from scratch). Combining a distinctive name with a clear, benefit-driven slogan solves both problems at once.

The Name + Slogan Formula: If your business name is abstract or modern (e.g., "NexaPay" or "AsoCraft"), your slogan should be literal and outcome-focused (e.g., "Instant Business Payments Across Nigeria"). If your business name is descriptive (e.g., "PrimeLagos Logistics"), your slogan can focus on emotion and trust (e.g., "Delivered On Time, Every Time").

4 Rules for Choosing a Business Name in Nigeria

Before printing packaging, designing a logo, or launching your website, test your shortlisted names against these four practical criteria:

  • 1. CAC Name Availability & Distinctiveness: The Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC) frequently rejects business name reservations that are too similar to existing registered companies or rely only on generic dictionary words. Pairing your core keyword with a distinctive prefix, compound word, or local root drastically improves approval odds.
  • 2. Domain Name & Social Handle Simplicity: Check whether the matching .com domain is available and easy to spell over a phone call. Avoid hyphens, numbers, or spellings that customers constantly mistype.
  • 3. Room to Grow: Avoid locking your brand into a single micro-product or single street name if you plan to expand later. A name like "CrestAgro Foods" scales much better across multiple product lines than "Ikeja Plantain Chips Enterprise".
  • 4. Bank Account & Trust Alignment: Nigerian buyers feel significantly safer paying into a corporate bank account whose name matches the website and invoice header they are interacting with.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use these generated business names for CAC registration?

Yes. All names and slogans generated by this tool are royalty-free for you to use. Because CAC requires two proposed names when filing a Name Reservation, we recommend saving your top 2 to 4 choices into your shortlist and running a quick availability search on the CAC public portal.

Why does the tool suggest .com domain names?

A .com domain is the universal gold standard for credibility, memorability, and global reach. Customers naturally type .com when looking up a brand online, making it the strongest primary domain for your business website and professional email.

Are my keywords or shortlisted brand names saved on your server?

No. Everything runs locally inside your browser. Your keywords and saved shortlist are never transmitted to any server and are automatically wiped from browser memory after 30 minutes.
